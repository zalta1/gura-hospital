# Programs & Services

Page subtitle: "Coordinated programs built around one condition at a time, not one department at a time."

If unsure which program fits: "Our access team will route you to the right specialist, usually within one business day."

## Structural Heart Program

A joint cardiology and cardiac surgery team evaluating valve disease together in a single visit, with transcatheter and surgical options reviewed side by side.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): 410 valve procedures last year

## Joint Replacement Center

Hip and knee replacement with a dedicated pre-op class, one nurse navigator per patient and a median discharge of 1.2 days.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): 94% same-day mobilization

## Stroke Response Team

Neurology, imaging and pharmacy activate on a single page, with CT completed before the patient leaves the resuscitation bay.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): 28-minute median door-to-needle

## Birth Center

Twelve labor suites, midwifery and OB care under one roof, on-site level III NICU and lactation support for twelve weeks postpartum.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): 2,300 births per year

## Imaging & Diagnostics

3T MRI, low-dose CT, echocardiography and interventional radiology with next-day appointments at the Lakeview campus.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): Results read within 24 hours

## Rehabilitation Services

Inpatient and outpatient physical, occupational and speech therapy, including cardiac and pulmonary rehab cohorts.

Stat (PLACEHOLDER): 18 therapy suites
